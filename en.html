Privacy Policy

Created by Karim G · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Your tools run on your device.

Navaja works offline, contains no ads or trackers, and does not send personal data to the developer or external servers.

1. Application and developer

This policy applies to Navaja, the Android tools application created by Karim G, with application ID cl.navaja.app. It explains what information the app uses on your device and how you can control it.

Navaja does not require an account, name, email address or registration to use its tools.

2. Information used by the app

Navaja does not collect data for transmission outside your device. Some features need to process or store information locally:

  • Calculator: stores your last 50 calculations and their results in the app's private storage. You can delete this history from the calculator.
  • Unit converter and birthday tool: the values and date you enter are used to perform calculations on your device. Your date of birth is not sent to any server.
  • Camera: displays a preview and lets you take photos. Photographs are saved in Pictures/Navaja, within your device's media storage. The app remembers a reference to the last photo so it can open it when you request it.
  • Compass and level: use the available sensors to calculate orientation and tilt. They do not use GPS location or keep a history of sensor readings. Level calibration is stored locally.
  • Clock, battery and system information: display information provided by Android, such as the date, time, battery percentage and temperature, device model, system version and display characteristics. This information is not sent to the developer.
  • Preferences: the theme, language and certain settings, such as the selected camera, are stored locally by the app or Android.
  • Calendar: displays dates and does not access your events, accounts or personal calendars.

3. Permissions and device controls

Navaja requests camera permission to take photographs and control the flashlight. You can deny or revoke this permission in Android settings; features that do not require it remain available.

When no flash is available, the flashlight can display a white screen at maximum brightness only after your confirmation. This adjustment applies to Navaja's window and is restored when you turn off or leave this feature.

The music buttons send previous, play/pause and next commands to the system. Navaja does not read the song title, your notifications or your music library.

The app does not request Internet, location, microphone, contacts, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi permissions.

4. Transmission and third-party access

Navaja does not sell data, include advertising, analytics or its own crash-reporting services, or operate its own servers or synchronization services.

If you choose to open a photograph, Android passes it to the corresponding gallery or image viewer. Saved photographs may be accessible to other applications you have granted permission to use them. If you use a gallery with cloud backup, that feature depends on its settings and privacy policy; Navaja does not perform that upload.

Android, Google Play and any external applications you choose to use manage their own services under their respective policies.

5. Retention and deletion

  • History: remains on your device until you delete it, clear Navaja's app data or uninstall the app. When the history exceeds 50 calculations, the oldest calculation is removed.
  • Preferences and calibration: you can reset them using the available controls or by clearing the app's data in Android. The language can also be managed through Android's per-app language settings.
  • Photos: remain in your device's media storage until you delete them using a gallery or file manager. Uninstalling Navaja does not delete these photographs or copies you have made using other services.

The developer does not receive this local information and cannot access or delete it remotely. Navaja has no user accounts that need to be deleted.

6. Information protection

History and preferences are stored in the app's private storage, subject to Android's protections. Photographs are saved in the device's shared media storage. Navaja does not create its own backups or add network connections to process this information.

7. This privacy policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its content does not add cookies, forms, analytics tools or tracking resources. When a GitHub Pages site is visited, GitHub logs the visitor's IP address for security purposes, as described in its privacy statement. This relates to the hosting of this website and is separate from Navaja's offline operation.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or privacy in Navaja, contact Karim G at karim.g.development@gmail.com.

If you voluntarily send an email, the developer will receive your email address and the information included in your message to respond to your inquiry. This correspondence will be retained for as long as necessary to handle the inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Navaja's features or information handling practices change. The current version will be published on this page with its update date.
